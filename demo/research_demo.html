How to read the replay

Ten labels on day one

Each held-out person provided five left and five right examples. The explorer shows the first archived calibration draw for each person. The paper averages 100 matched draws.

Four views of the same signal

Four linear classifiers measure spatial covariance, cross-spectral relationships, short time windows and rhythm envelopes. Their transformed scores receive equal weight. A right-hand score above 50% gives a right prediction. The score is an uncalibrated model output.

Two day-two protocols

Independent predictions use the calibration set and current trial. Chronological replay can also update normalization from earlier unlabeled trials. Each trial is scored before it enters the history. Settings were selected separately using source participants. Jumping between clips retrieves their original chronological predictions.

What you see

The trial comparison shows the archived final decisions immediately. Playing the trace illustrates the EEG that produced them. These are precomputed research predictions on recorded, cued imagery. The display shows three scalp channels resampled from 250 to 100 Hz. The classifiers used additional channels and features. Each decision used a completed epoch ending 4.5 seconds after the cue. Playback duration is recording time, not measured inference latency.
